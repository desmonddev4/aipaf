Membership

Join a profession as it is being built.

Membership is an invitation to be recognised for competence that meets a standard built for African practice, and to help shape a profession while it is still being formed.

Who it is for

The professionals whose work touches the delivery, assurance, or investigation of projects.

  • DeliverProject managers, engineers, quantity surveyors, architects, and builders.
  • AssureAuditors, reviewers, and advisers.
  • InvestigateForensic accountants, lawyers, and expert examiners.
  • Teach and learnThe academics who teach these disciplines and the students who will practise them next.

What membership offers

Standing, standards, and a place in the profession.

A recognised designation

The CPMP, CPAP, or CPFE, and the standing each carries with clients, employers, and funders.

A relevant standard

Certification built for the conditions African projects are actually delivered in.

Knowledge and development

The Institute's bodies of knowledge, examinations, and continuing professional development.

A place in the profession

A community of practitioners, and a voice in a profession being built.

A founding distinction

For early members, recognition as part of the founding cohort.

The grades

Membership grows with competence.

Only the certified grades carry the right to practise under the Institute's designations and to use the corresponding post-nominals.

GradeWho it is forRights
Fellow (FIPAF)Certified members of distinguished standing and sustained contribution.Full voting rights; eligible for Council; highest post-nominal.
Member (MIPAF)Fully certified practitioners holding CPMP, CPAP, or CPFE.Full voting rights; may practise under Institute designations.
Associate (AIPAF)Practitioners who have passed the foundation and are completing certification.Limited voting; defined practice under supervision.
AffiliateProfessionals in cognate fields who support the Institute's aims without seeking certification.Non-voting; access to events, research, and CPD.
StudentThose enrolled on a route towards certification.Non-voting; access to learning and mentoring.

Founding Membership Is Open

Help found the profession.

The Institute is now admitting its founding cohort. Those who join at this stage do so at the establishment of the profession, and are recognised as having helped to found it.

Prospective members, and organisations wishing to partner with the Institute, are invited to register their interest with the Secretariat.

Registering interest does not commit you to joining. The Secretariat will reply by email with the next steps.
