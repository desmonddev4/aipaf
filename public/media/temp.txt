How this register is to be usedThis document serves two related purposes and the distinction between them governs what may be filed.As a working register, it captures the full field of persons identified by the founding convenors as candidates for association with the Institute, whether as members of the profession, as honorary fellows, or as affiliates from adjacent disciplines. Candidates are drawn from the academic staff of the College of Art and Built Environment at KNUST, from the board, management and professional associates of Green Communities International, and from the academic and industry leaders who contributed to the International Conference on Environment, Social, Governance and Sustainable Development of Africa in 2024 and 2025.As a statutory schedule, only a subset may be filed. The Registrar treats the list of members as a representation that the persons named are members at the date of application. A person who has not signed a membership application and accepted the code of conduct is not a member, and the disciplinary jurisdiction asserted in the constitution cannot attach to that person. Every entry therefore carries a consent status, and the schedule annexed to Form A is generated as the extract of consented entries on the cut off date.The sequence is straightforward. Issue the invitation and application form. Record the signed acceptance. Change the consent status. Generate the filing extract. Nothing here should be filed ahead of that sequence.2. Membership classes and the coverage calculationSection 5(2) of NRCD 143 requires that the membership of a professional body represent at least seventy five per cent of all persons trained and qualified in the profession concerned. That is a ratio, and both terms of it move together. A qualified person who joins the Institute enters the numerator and the denominator; a qualified person who does not join enters the denominator alone. Enrolment is therefore never harmful. What is harmful is defining the profession so broadly that the denominator swells with practitioners the Institute has no claim to and no prospect of enrolling.The constitution accordingly separates the classes of association into those that fall within the profession and those that do not. Position on dispute resolution practiceThe two bodies most often assumed to occupy expert determination in Ghana do not, and neither is a registered professional body.The Chartered Institute of Arbitrators operates in Ghana through a Chapter formed in September 2022, administratively attached to the CIArb Nigeria Branch. It confers the grades of a United Kingdom charity and connects the dispute resolution community through events and training. It does not appear on the ORC register of professional bodies and asserts no jurisdiction over a Ghanaian profession. The Ghana Arbitration Centre is a private provider of arbitration services rather than a regulator, and likewise does not appear on the register.The Alternative Dispute Resolution Act, 2010 (Act 798) provides for the settlement of disputes by arbitration, mediation and customary arbitration and establishes an ADR Centre. Expert determination is treated in practitioner commentary as one of the recognised ADR mechanisms, but the operative provisions of Act 798 do not regulate it as a distinct process, and no body holds a statutory monopoly over it.The exposure that does exist is at registry level rather than statutory, and it comes from the Ghana National Association of ADR Practitioners, which is registered and in good standing. Dispute avoidance and expert determination have accordingly been removed from the definition of the profession on the face of this schedule. They are retained in the credential specification as competencies that Fellows may practise, which preserves the substance while removing the only credible ground of objection from an incumbent body.11. Remaining matter to settleOne check remains and it is the one that governs everything else. Establish the cumulative graduate count across the MSc Project Forensics, MSc Value Management and MSc Project Cost Engineering and Management programmes. If the total qualified population falls below fifty, the application requires presidential dispensation under the fifty person proviso in NRCD 143, and the founding convenors should decide whether to seek it or to defer filing until a further cohort has graduated.
