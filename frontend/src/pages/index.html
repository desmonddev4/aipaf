The Mandate

Africa does not lack projects. It lacks the assurance that they will deliver, and the means to establish why when they do not.

Across the continent, too many projects stall, overrun, or fail for reasons that were foreseeable and preventable. AIPAF exists to change that: to build the profession that delivers projects to a sound standard, reviews them independently before the money is committed, and investigates them honestly when they fall short.

Read the mandate
  • DeliverProject management to an assurance-grade standard, built for African conditions.
  • AssureIndependent oversight of a project's likely performance, before it proceeds.
  • InvestigateForensic inquiry into failure and dispute, to a standard that withstands scrutiny.

The Credentials

Three certified designations. One shared standard.

Each designation is earned through a shared professional foundation, examined track papers, and verified practical experience.

CPFE

Certified Project Forensic Examiner

Forensics

Investigate project failure and dispute to an evidentiary standard capable of surviving independent scrutiny.

CPAP

Certified Project Assurance Practitioner

Assurance

Provide independent, forward-looking oversight of a project's likely performance for those who sanction, finance, or insure it.

View requirements (PDF) →

CPMP

Certified Project Management Practitioner

Delivery

The African project management standard: deliver projects to a governance-ready, auditable standard, built to compete with the global credentials on relevance.

View requirements (PDF) →

Delivery and independent scrutiny are kept strictly separate. A member may not assure or investigate a project they have delivered. That separation is what makes the Institute's judgement worth relying on.

The Pathway

From Certified to Chartered.

Certified

Earned by examination

The qualification to practise, earned through the shared foundation, the track examinations, and verified experience. CPMP, CPAP, or CPFE.

Chartered

Senior standing

The senior professional standing above Certified, conferred on members of sustained distinction once the Institute holds the necessary chartering authority.

Membership

A profession you can build a career in.

Membership grows with your competence, from first enrolment to the highest grade of the Institute.

  1. GraduateEnrolled on a route towards certification.
  2. AffiliateCognate professionals who support the Institute's aims.
  3. Associate (AAIPAF)Foundation passed, certification in progress.
  4. Member (MAIPAF)Fully certified and practising under a designation.
  5. Fellow (FAIPAF)Distinguished standing and sustained contribution.

Graduate Member

For those enrolled on a route towards certification, including doctoral candidates and early-career researchers in the field.

Requirements
  • Enrolled in or pursuing relevant study in project assurance, delivery, or forensics
  • No experience requirement (in training)
  • No examination requirement initially

Graduate membership provides access to learning and mentoring while members work towards certification.

Affiliate Member

For qualified professionals in adjacent disciplines who engage with AIPAF's work.

Requirements
  • Qualified in an adjacent discipline (e.g., law, accounting, engineering, architecture)
  • Engagement with AIPAF's work or objectives
  • No initial experience requirement
  • Examinations optional (can pursue certification tracks)

Affiliate membership is for professionals who support the Institute's aims and may wish to pursue certification in the future.

Associate Member (AAIPAF)

For practitioners actively working in the profession, pending full assessment of qualifications.

Requirements
  • Practising in project management, assurance, or forensics
  • Practical experience in the domain
  • Foundation examination passed
  • Track examination in progress or pending

Associate membership is for practitioners who have begun the certification pathway and are working towards full Member grade.

Member (MAIPAF)

For fully certified practitioners with established professional standing.

Requirements
  • Doctoral qualification OR AIPAF credential (CPMP, CPAP, or CPFE)
  • Established practice in the profession
  • Relevant practical experience in the domain
  • Foundation examination + track examination completed
  • CPD: Maintain continuing professional development

Member grade is the full professional grade for certified practitioners operating under an AIPAF designation.

Fellow (FAIPAF)

For distinguished practitioners with professorial rank or equivalent senior standing.

Requirements
  • Professorial rank or equivalent standing in the profession
  • Teaching, examining, or senior practice in project assurance and forensics
  • Established senior practice (minimum 10 years)
  • Foundation + track examination + Chartered assessment
  • Sustained contribution to the profession or Institute

Fellowship is the highest grade of the Institute, conferred on members of distinguished standing and sustained contribution.

Why AIPAF

Built for African conditions. Answerable on the continent.

Independence

Our members opine free of the interests of those who deliver, finance, or benefit from a project. Independence is what makes the Institute's opinions worth relying on.

Evidence

Judgements rest on facts gathered and weighed by sound method, not on assertion, reputation, or pressure. What cannot be evidenced is not opined.

African ownership

A standard built for African delivery conditions, governed on the continent, and answerable to the people whose projects it concerns.
