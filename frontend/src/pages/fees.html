Concessions

Founding cohort: no application fee and half the annual subscription for the first fee year. Retired members aged 60+ who are no longer in practice pay 40% of the grade rate. Admission on or after 1 July: half the annual subscription. Council may grant hardship relief.

Subscription dates

Subscriptions fall due 1 January and are payable by 31 January. Membership lapses on 31 March. Lapse beyond five years is treated as a fresh application.

Free services

Register searches, certificate verification, scheme rules and standards, false-claim reports, complaints, and successful appeals are free.

Schedule rules: Fees are published and not negotiated; applicants on the same scale pay the same. A refusal is charged because assessment is the service provided. Fee income is not a ground for granting or withholding a certificate. No fee may be contingent on an outcome. Fees in an accepted written estimate do not change.

Payment and tax: Payment may be by bank transfer, mobile money or card; no cash or payment to an individual account is accepted. The schedule identifies VAT treatment as a publication item to settle with tax advice. Confirm VAT and applicable currency with the Secretariat before payment. A future schedule change is to be published 90 days before taking effect.
