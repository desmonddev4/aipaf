Data protection

Privacy notice.

How the Institute collects, uses, stores, and deletes personal information submitted through this website.

Purpose of collection

The Institute uses information submitted through the contact and founding-membership forms to respond to enquiries, assess interest, and maintain the records needed to administer the Institute.

Consent is required. You must confirm that you agree to this use before a submission is accepted.

Information collected

Depending on the form, AIPAF may collect name, email address, organisation, country, professional area, message content, submission page, and the time taken to submit the form.

Storage and retention

  • Submissions are stored in a protected PostgreSQL database.
  • Records are retained only while needed for administration, follow-up, and legal or regulatory requirements.
  • Contact and membership records should normally be reviewed and marked handled within a defined operational period.
  • Records are deleted when they are no longer necessary, except where law or professional obligations require their retention.

Access and deletion

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, contact the Institute by email at info@aipaf.africa. Please include enough detail to identify the relevant submission.

The Institute will acknowledge requests and may need to verify your identity before disclosing or deleting personal data.

Security

Administrative access is limited to authorised Secretariat and Council users. Credentials must be supplied through secure Vercel environment variables and must never be committed to the repository.

Information for Ghanaian residents

This privacy notice is intended to support compliance with the Ghana Data Protection Act, 2012. Processing is based on consent, legitimate Institute interests, and legal or professional obligations where applicable.
